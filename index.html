My ESP 103 learning journey

From curiosity to entrepreneurship

01 · Curiosity

A charged quest for comprehension. Ask questions, seek novelty, and notice problems worth solving.

02 · Creativity

Generate novel, useful possibilities. Mini-c, little-c, Pro-c and Big-C describe different scales of creative contribution.

03 · Creative confidence

Believe you can generate ideas and act on them. Practice, feedback and a growth mindset help overcome fear of judgment.

04 · Resilience

Adapt when things go wrong. Emotional regulation, optimism, cognitive agility, self-compassion and self-efficacy help turn setbacks into learning.

05 · Innovation

Move from ideas to experiences people can use. Balance desirability, feasibility and viability.

06 · Entrepreneurship

Test an opportunity, build a solution, and learn from risk, constraints and failure.

Research and interpretation

Creativity emerges from interactions across large-scale networks, not one isolated “creativity center.” Mental health diagnoses are not creative superpowers. Research associations vary by study, symptom severity, treatment and environment; individual experiences differ.

Further reading: Review on creativity and psychopathology · Association for Psychological Science: mixed evidence on mood disorders · NIMH mental health topics
